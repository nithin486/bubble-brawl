Bubblaze: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Contact: %CONTACT%

Bubblaze has no accounts of its own and asks for no personal details.

Stored on your device

Your progress, coins, settings, the player name you type and a short log of game events are kept in your browser or app storage. We do not upload them to a server of ours. Clearing the site or app data deletes them.

Playing with friends

To connect a room, the game uses the PeerJS connection service and, when needed, relay servers. Players then connect to each other. The connection services and the other players in your room can learn your IP address, and the others see your player name, hero and items. Share room links only with people you know.

Delivering the game

Like any website, the hosting providers (Cloudflare and GitHub, or the game portal you play on) receive your IP address and basic request details.

Advertising

Some versions show videos or ads supplied by the game portal (for example CrazyGames) or by Google. Those providers may collect device and usage information under their own privacy policies. The Android app version shows no ads.

Purchases

Purchases in the Android app are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; the game only receives a purchase reference so it can give you the items.

On CrazyGames

When you play on CrazyGames, your progress may be saved to your CrazyGames account and your CrazyGames user name may be used as your player name.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and requests

Changes will appear on this page. For any question or request, use the contact above.
